LEH (standing for "Liam Executable Header") is 16 bytes long and consists of the following data types and values:

uint8_t headerStart     = 0xFF
uint32_t signature1     = "LSOS"
uint16_t signature2     = "EH"
uint16_t filler         = 0x0000
uint8_t idk             = 0x03
uint32_t filler         = 0x00000000
uint8_t headerEnd       = 0xFF
